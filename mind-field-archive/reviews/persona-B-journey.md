## First-visit summary

**What I think the site is for.** It is an archive of a workshop run by "MIND FIELD". It shows the content of two talks: 1부 "AI 시대의 변화" (what AI can and cannot do) and 2부 "에이전트와 만드는 법" (how to build with agents). A "다리" (bridge) connects them. The 사례관 (showcase) collects 35 examples of things made with frontier AI models. It is laid out like a big exhibition wall that you pan and zoom around.

**What I understood and what I did not.** I understood that this is a gallery of talk material, and that you can go in from a poster, a list (목록) or a spotlight tour (도슨트). I also understood the shortcuts, but only after I found the shortcuts window by accident. I did not understand most of the content. The words 하네스, 루프, 그래프, 컨텍스트 엔지니어링, 프롬프트 엔지니어링 and 에이전트 were never explained where I met them. The glossary of "바이브코딩 용어 17" did help, but I only found it by scrolling past it. The tags 확정 / 단서 / 정정 / 미확인 and "감사 범위 밖" were not explained. Raw file paths such as "refs/drive/..." and ":155" line numbers are all over the screen, and they felt like internal notes rather than something for visitors. I also kept losing control, because the camera keeps moving when I click and Esc only goes back one level at a time.

**What I'd tell a friend.** "Visually it's the coolest archive page I've seen. The black and neon-green layout, the big Korean serif type and the zoom-in-from-a-wall idea are great. But it's a lecture archive for people who already know the talk, so you won't really learn how to build anything from it. The showcase page is the fun part, and the glossary is useful. On a phone it is broken."

**Would it help me build my commission portfolio site?** Only a little. It inspired me visually: the poster-wall idea, the spotlight (도슨트) mode and the scroll-site example in the showcase are things I would like to borrow for my gallery. But it never shows how to build anything. I saw no step-by-step guide and no prompts I could copy. I saw nothing about forms, prices, booking slots or a status tracker. The finished examples have only an "X 원본" link and no "how it was made". I would take the glossary and the visual mood from it, and look for a real tutorial somewhere else to build the commission service.
