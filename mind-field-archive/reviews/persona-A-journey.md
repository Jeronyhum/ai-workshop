## First-visit summary

**What I think it is.** A showcase archive from a workshop about AI: part 1 is data about how AI is changing work and jobs, part 2 is "how to work with AI agents" (prompt, context, harness, loop, graph), and a gallery of examples people made with the newest models. It looks like an exhibition more than a tutorial.

**What I understood / didn't.** I understood the glossary ("바이브코딩 용어 17" — best thing I found), and the message "AI states wrong numbers confidently, so build verification in" (다리 A). The 하네스/루프/그래프 pieces I only half followed: nice animations but I don't know what to actually do. I never found a step-by-step "how to build an app with AI" and nothing about member lists, sign-ups, attendance, dues or deployment (only the word 호스팅 in the glossary). The first screen never says what the site is or who it is for. Navigation confused me: clicks on 사례관 and 목록 seemed delayed, opened the wrong thing, or did nothing; I couldn't find a plain list. Lots of English in the gallery. The phone version is basically unusable. I liked the honest labels ("확정 / 미확인", source lines), though I didn't know what "감사" referred to.

**What I'd tell a friend.** "예쁘게 만든 AI 워크숍 전시 사이트야. 용어 사전이랑 '검증하는 습관' 얘기는 쓸 만한데, 앱 만드는 튜토리얼을 기대하면 안 돼. 컴퓨터로 보고, 처음엔 헤맬 거야."

**Would it help me build my club app?** A little, indirectly. The glossary gives me words (GitHub, hosting, API, token) to use when I talk to an AI tool, and the "verify AI numbers" idea matters for dues and receipts. But it doesn't show me how to start, what to ask for, or what to watch out for with member data. I'd bookmark the glossary and the spreadsheet example, then go back to ChatGPT. It would help much more with a "start here for building an app" path in Korean on the first screen.
